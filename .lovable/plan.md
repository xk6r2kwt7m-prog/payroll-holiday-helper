# Allergen Training audit — read-only report

Nothing was changed, published, assigned or sent. No emails, no notifications, no test data.

Headline: there are **three separate things** that all look like "allergen training", and they are not joined up.

1. **Allergen Training tab** (Documents & Compliance) — a document and content governance area for you. It produces an approved, versioned wording set. It has no learner.
2. **General training system** (Training screen) — a real working learner system (assign, lessons, quiz, pass mark, retries, manager sign-off), but its allergen modules are old and are **not** fed by the approved allergen wording.
3. **Static FOH allergy pages** (`/foh/allergy`, `/foh/print`) — fixed, hard-coded pages with no version control, no assignment and no record of who read them.

---

## A. What already works

**Allergen governance (admin/manager)**
- Source library with your five-level priority order, current/historical flag, version and date. 13 sources on record (3 current operational procedures, 10 historical).
- "Review uploaded documents" scans uploaded compliance documents, registers them as unranked sources awaiting your rank, and drafts proposals. It never edits course content directly.
- Flavour reference: 32 flavours, 27 with site availability set from the July 2026 menus, all still `unconfirmed` pending the approved matrix.
- Conflicts: disagreements are recorded with each side's wording, rank and date, a recommended wording, and a "needs your decision" flag when the rules cannot separate two sources. 3 conflicts, all resolved as "reference only".
- Proposals: approve / reject / send back, per item or in bulk, with who decided and when.
- Publishing: insert-only. Published version 1 (18 Sep 2026) contains 16 lessons, 17 questions, 32 flavour lines, 5 practical sign-off points, an emergency procedure, and a source map linking every statement and question back to its source documents. 38 approved proposals recorded against it. Previous versions can never be edited or deleted.
- Every action is written to the compliance audit trail.

**General training system (works today, for other modules)**
- Manager assigns a module to individuals (tick-list), a department, or all unassigned staff; optional due date; duplicates auto-skipped; confirmation dialog showing module, headcount, source and deadline before confirming.
- Staff see their assignments on their own account, grouped Overdue / Due soon / Failed / In progress / Awaiting sign-off / Not started / Completed, with a progress percentage.
- Lessons on screen (for modules that have lesson content), then quiz: one question at a time, progress bar, pass mark, attempt limit (default 3), lock-out after the last attempt, per-question explanation on review, full attempt history kept.
- Manager sign-off, module evidence and effectiveness tracking; in-app notification on assignment; training audit log.
- Certificate and licence expiry reminders already exist as a scheduled job (in-app and email) for `compliance_certificates`.

## B. What is partly built

- **Lesson content**: exists for a handful of hard-coded modules only; section ordering, estimated minutes, mandatory-section gating and save-and-continue exist in the **induction document reader**, not in the training lesson viewer.
- **Learning before the test**: enforced only when a module happens to have lesson content; otherwise staff can open the quiz directly.
- **Completion records**: assignments store score, pass/fail, module version, sign-off. `training_records` (manual certificate entries) has 0 rows; no certificate is generated by the system.
- **Branch/role targeting**: fields exist (`target_roles`, `target_locations`, branch ids on flavours) but assignment is by individual/department/all only — not by branch or role.
- **Reporting**: a completion dashboard with status counts and CSV export exists; it is not filterable by branch, manager, course version or certificate status.

## E. Current staff experience

A member of staff with an account sees, on their own login: the two assigned items above, the static FOH allergy pages (reference only, no record kept), and any module a manager assigns. They cannot see the approved allergen version 1 anywhere. No secure link exists for someone without an account. Progress is remembered between visits for assignments and induction reading; the quiz itself is not saved mid-way — closing it loses the answers. The layout works on a phone; the admin allergen review screen is desktop-shaped and awkward on a phone. Learner screens are English-only, even though the app supports Portuguese elsewhere.

## F. Current administrator experience

Full control of the governance loop: rank sources, review drafts, resolve conflicts, approve wording, publish a version — and publishing contacts nobody. But after publishing there is no next step: no way to turn version 1 into something staff must read and pass, and no certificate at the end.

## G. Risks and conflicts

- Two competing allergen modules already in the library ("Allergen Awareness" with 38 questions, "Allergy Safety Guide" with none) plus the static FOH pages. Staff could pass a quiz whose content is older than approved version 1, with no link to a source document.
- All 32 flavours are unconfirmed until the approved allergen matrix is uploaded, so no dish-specific question can be safely scored yet.
- The FOH pages are open to any signed-in member of staff with no training-module or permission check.
- The existing 2 assignments and 5 food-safety certificates must be left untouched by any future build.

## H. Recommended build phases (not implemented)

1. Approved version → staff course: generate a module and structured, markable questions from version 1, keeping every source link. Nothing auto-assigned.
2. Exam integrity: critical questions, pass mark plus all-critical-correct, randomised options, branch/current-menu filtering, learning-before-test gate, saved answers.
3. Assignment and tracking: branch/role targeting, exclusions, deadlines, cancel, reassign, recipient preview, full status set and filters.
4. Certificates: auto-issue on exam pass plus practical sign-off, unique reference, version, score, renewal date, stored on the employee record and in Certificates & Expiry, preserved when a new version publishes.
5. Reminders and inspection export, manual-first, off until you switch each one on.
6. Mobile, accessibility and translated learner content.
