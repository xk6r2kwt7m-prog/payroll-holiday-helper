# Improve and standardise Table Service Steps

## Goal
Turn the supplied notes into a polished, phone-friendly Ugly Dumpling training document that follows the same structure, source labels and approval controls as the existing induction lessons.

## Content update
- Keep the existing eight-step order: Warm Welcome; Reservations and Walk-ins; Order Taking; Homemade Sauces and Chopsticks; Check-ins; Food Delivery and Explanation; Suggestions; Bill and Feedback.
- Rewrite for clear British English, short action-led instructions and consistent terminology, while preserving the operational meaning supplied.
- Correct unclear wording such as “eyes contact”, “allergens information”, “time of wait” and “allergens air special dietary requirements”.
- Separate safety-critical allergen actions from general service guidance so they are visually prominent and cannot be mistaken for optional suggestions.
- Do not invent menu items, timing rules, legal claims or service procedures that are not supported by the supplied material or an already approved Ugly Dumpling source.

## Alignment with existing training
- Add **Table Service Steps** as its own versioned, source-backed Front of House lesson using the existing lesson format: Overview, Why This Matters, Step-by-Step Standard, Common Mistakes, Service Scenarios, Learning Outcomes and manager observation points.
- Classify the supplied service process as an **Internal Company Standard** and retain a clear source reference for every teaching point.
- Add the lesson to the existing Front of House induction pack as a new draft version. It will remain unavailable to staff until separately approved through the existing approval process.
- Keep the current standalone Service Steps page and printable guide aligned to the same wording, avoiding two conflicting versions.

## Presentation
- Keep each step easy to scan on a phone, with timing shown only where the source states it.
- Use the existing training page style and controls rather than introducing a new page or visual system.
- Preserve the printable guide and its confidentiality notice.

## Safeguards and checks
- Do not publish, assign, email or notify staff.
- Do not change existing completions, approvals or historical lesson versions.
- Test that the lesson is registered, source-linked, included only in the Front of House pack, held back while unapproved, and rendered correctly in both the on-screen and printable guides.
- Check the page at phone and desktop sizes and confirm the existing training tests and preview remain healthy.
